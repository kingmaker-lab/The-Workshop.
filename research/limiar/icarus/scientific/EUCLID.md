# ATH-002 EUCLID | formalization draft v0.1
Code inspected: exp00x-icaro/icaro.c blob 4cb93c558d22e356b14d7e0df300dca805b8ca20. Model assumes n<=256, constant announced integer b in {8,16,24,32}, base c=4, increment d=28, finite input, optional expansion cap K.
Let P_i={1,...,i}, w_i=abs(sum of first four features), S selected for expansion. C_i=4i+28|S intersect P_i|. Define q_i=floor(i(b-4)/28); Q_i=min(q_i,K) for capped replay, otherwise Q_i=q_i. Feasible if |S intersect P_i|<=Q_i for every i. Q_i is nondecreasing with increments at most 1. Objective lexicographic: maximize |S| then minimize sum w_i (tie prefer smaller indices). This is not an accuracy-optimality claim.
## Theorem 1 (logical budget)
At scan i, insert index i, then remove the largest (w,index) if heap size >Q_i. Induction: at step i all earlier prefix constraints hold because insertion only changes the current prefix, and removal cannot increase any prefix count. Heap size equals Q_i by induction since Q_0=0 and increment is 0 or 1. Future insertions have higher index and removals only decrease prior counts. Therefore the final S is feasible and has maximum feasible cardinality Q_n. C_i<=ib; sum over independent batches implies aggregate logical budget. For b=16,n=64 maximum terms=4*64+28*floor(64*12/28)=1012, unused=12.
## Theorem 2 (minimum total margin at maximum feasible cardinality)
Stronger induction: for every k<=Q_i, the k smallest elements of current heap are a minimum-weight feasible k-set in P_i. Base empty. A feasible k-set in P_i either excludes i (possible if k<=Q_(i-1)) or includes i (requires a feasible k-1 set in P_(i-1) and k<=Q_i). By induction these two alternatives have optimum sums from the smallest k or k-1 previous heap elements. Taking k smallest of previous heap union {i} chooses the smaller sum among feasible alternatives; trimming the largest to Q_i does not affect any such k. Thus result at k=Q_n is optimal. Deterministic ties follow lexicographic element ordering. The constraints also form a chain of laminar sets (Fife & Oxley 2017, see ALEXANDRIA).
## Complexity and scope
Heap O(n log(n+1)) time, O(n) auxiliary storage, bounded batch size. Native validation scans all 32 features; term count excludes heap, copies, checks, observation and input acquisition. Prefix guarantee is logical, not causal wall-clock or CPU quota. Full batch lookahead required. Smaller margin is a heuristic for benefit; no proof of accuracy, energy or latency. Uniform incremental cost required; heterogeneous costs not covered. No theorem of OS safety: telemetry sampled, not continuous.
