# ATLAS-02 KINESIS | development-only study
MOTION observed computational savings but missed the joint target; this study investigates hierarchical classification and learned signed benefit. No reads of official test files: nine MOTION test subjects excluded. Original UCI HAR official train only (7,352 overlapping windows, 21 participants). Fixed shuffled 3-fold development CV, each fold holds out 7 people, splits remaining 14 into 8 classifier fit, 3 benefit fit, 3 threshold tune. Each subject evaluated once; subjects may appear in training for other folds. This is not an untouched external cohort, no confirmatory claim; previous MOTION informed design. Smoke first 2 held-development batches of fold 0 only, excluded from any added sample count.

Features: 6 per-axis means and 6 variances cheap; 18 lag 1/8/32 mean squared differences only on expansion. Same 30 features as MOTION reordered. Native flat full baseline standardized 6-class centroid (same model), hierarchical gate nearest equal-class-weight group centroid using 12 cheap features, then nearest of three group classes. Group fixed even after expansion, so group errors cannot be repaired; record coarse accuracy. Hier full and lite share gate; compare flat full to expose gate cost and error, not just weaker baseline. Model fitted only on fit subjects. Distances normalized by pooled fit variance floor 1e-8. Within-group margin = (second-first)/(1+second+first). Signed gain g=I(hier full correct)-I(hier lite correct). Bin margin at .02,.05,.10,.20,.40,.60; pooled shrinkage sum(g)/(n+16), group-bin score (sum(g)+32*pooled)/(n+32), using benefit-fit people only. Independently choose thresholds per q on tune people: margin 5% quantiles plus extremes; benefit all unique table values plus adjacent below and extremes; maximize pooled correct, ties smallest threshold. No eval labels used for selection. Benefit and margin constrained to exact floor(n*q/4) expansions with prefix floor(i*q/4), q=1,2,3; forced final fill even for negative predicted gain. STATIC identical expansion counts. Six arms FLAT_FULL, HIER_FULL, HIER_LITE, STATIC, MARGIN, BENEFIT. Contiguous source-order batches <=64 without crossing subject. Not necessarily chronological. Five randomized paired repetitions; native CPU includes actual feature extraction and output, excludes allocation, packing, Python, parsing, training, acquisition, filtering and I/O. Compare geometric subject ratios of sums of median batch CPU relative to both full references. Record masks, margins, coarse groups, confusion matrices and per-subject outcomes. No formal significance or noninferiority; repeated windows overlap and folds share training data. Do not tune on evaluation outcomes and call it confirmatory. Independent dataset/architecture and per-policy memory remain future work.

Safety guard same passive 256 MiB/80 C from MOTION, sampled per batch/repetition and fit; no pressure, sudo or new deps. Input native buffer 384 KiB, Python allocations additional. Atomic hashed fold model and batch checkpoints; manifest locks revision, source hashes and native binary. Resume same invocation command, no git pull/recompile in between. Smoke and pilot need distinct checkpoints. Local compile/execution pending.
