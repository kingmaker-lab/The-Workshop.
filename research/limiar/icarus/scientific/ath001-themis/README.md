All use four-batch feedback delay. Target strata for two audits [2,32),[32,64), for four [2,16),[16,32),[32,48),[48,64). Draw one in each, with 25% uniform +75% uniform over plain policy disagreement if nonempty, otherwise uniform. Actual propensity recorded; use inverse probability HT estimate of plain-policy gain, not audited policy gain. No future labels for decisions, fixed model from calibration, all audit expansion costs included in 27. Keep original choice until 16 delivered batch estimates; require >=4 nonzero batch estimates and mean +/- one sample SE wholly above +0.25 pp or below -0.25 pp to switch. Otherwise retain incumbent. SE is a heuristic, not a valid sequential confidence bound. Adaptive count starts 2, escalates to 4 when estimated signal ambiguous or sparse, otherwise 2. No-feedback freezes policy and audits 2 for THEMIS. Model refit deferred to avoid conflating effects and biased targeted training.
